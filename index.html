Mental Wellness Guides & Support

school

Academic Stress Management

  • Break study sessions into 25-minute Pomodoro intervals.
  • Set realistic goals based on your own growth, not comparative expectations.
  • Grades measure specific test outcomes, not your intrinsic human value.
  • Communicate proactively with mentors and academic counselors.
family_restroom

Family Dynamics & Boundaries

  • Practice calm, assertive communication during disagreements.
  • Establish healthy boundaries with emotional empathy.
  • Identify at least one supportive relative or trusted elder.
  • Introduce your aspirations step-by-step with mutual respect.
public

Navigating Societal & Social Pressures

  • Separate personal identity from external societal scrutiny ("Log kya kahenge").
  • Limit toxic social media scrolling; curate positive, realistic feeds.
  • Build a supportive peer group aligned with your true interests.
  • Celebrate non-linear learning journeys and diverse career paths.
spa

Physical & Emotional Equilibrium

  • Prioritize 7-8 hours of regular, uninterrupted sleep.
  • Incorporate 20-30 minutes of moderate physical movement daily.
  • Practice 5-10 minutes of box breathing or mindful grounding.
  • Stay well hydrated and maintain consistent meal timing.
medical_services

When to Consult a Professional

Professional therapy is an empowering step towards lasting strength and clarity.

  • Persistent feelings of numbness, sadness, or overwhelming anxiety.
  • Significant disruption in sleep, appetite, or focus lasting over two weeks.
  • Intense feelings of isolation or difficulty carrying out daily routines.
  • Any thoughts of hopelessness or self-harm (Reach out immediately).
